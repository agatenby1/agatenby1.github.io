All cases are fictional and written for teaching. No real patient, consultation or record has been used, adapted or anonymised in any tool on this site.

References orient you to the evidence; they are not a substitute for local pathways or your own senior review. Where answers are defensible positions rather than the only correct ones, the tool says so.
